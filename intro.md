# Raipur-UHI-Mapping-Project
A ground-level sensor network measuring hyperlocal temperature variation across 8 urban zones in Raipur, India — correlating street-level heat data with Landsat 9 satellite NDVI to produce the city's first neighbourhood-scale urban heat island map. Built with Arduino + DHT22 sensors. Mentored by NIT Raipur.
